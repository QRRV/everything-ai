# Leftovers in the fridge

Put leftovers in the fridge within two hours after cooking, in a closed box. They keep this many days:

| food | days in the fridge |
|---|---|
| cooked rice | 1 |
| cooked pasta | 3 |
| soup | 3 |
| pancakes | 2 |
| rice pudding | 2 |
| apple pie | 4 |

When in doubt, throw it away.
