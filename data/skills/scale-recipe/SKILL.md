# A recipe for another number of people

Every recipe of this kitchen is for 4 people.

1. Divide the number of people the user cooks for by 4.
2. Multiply each amount of the recipe by that number.
3. Give the amounts in whole grams. Round eggs up to whole eggs.

Example: for 2 people, 250 grams of flour becomes 125 grams.
