# Oven settings

The recipes of this kitchen give the temperature for an oven without a fan.

For a fan oven: take 20 degrees off.

For a gas oven, use the gas mark:

| degrees | gas mark |
|---|---|
| 140 | 1 |
| 150 | 2 |
| 160 | 3 |
| 180 | 4 |
| 190 | 5 |
| 200 | 6 |
| 220 | 7 |
| 230 | 8 |
