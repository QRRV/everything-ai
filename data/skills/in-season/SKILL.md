# In season in the Netherlands

| month | in season |
|---|---|
| January to March | leeks, kale, carrots, apples, pears |
| April and May | asparagus, rhubarb, spinach |
| June to August | strawberries, cherries, tomatoes, courgettes, beans |
| September and October | apples, pears, pumpkins, plums |
| November and December | kale, leeks, Brussels sprouts, apples |

Anything not in the row of a month is not in season then.
