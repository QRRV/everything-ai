# Cups and spoons in grams

This kitchen weighs everything in grams. Use these numbers, not others:

| ingredient | 1 cup | 1 tablespoon |
|---|---|---|
| flour | 125 grams | 8 grams |
| sugar | 200 grams | 12 grams |
| butter | 225 grams | 14 grams |
| milk | 240 grams | 15 grams |
| rice | 185 grams | 12 grams |

1. Find the ingredient in the table.
2. Multiply its grams by the number of cups or tablespoons.
3. Give the answer in whole grams.
